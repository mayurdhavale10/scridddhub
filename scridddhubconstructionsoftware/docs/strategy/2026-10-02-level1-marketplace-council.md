# Level 1 as a land marketplace? Council review (2026-10-02)

**Question (owner):** should Level 1 open up so landowners, sellers and others store their land,
RERA and legal data on ScridddHub, with public information gathered automatically, and should
ScridddHub earn commission like a real-estate agent?

**Method:** four independent researchers (market, legal, economics, skeptic), each citing
sources, then a combined verdict. Not legal advice; items marked [Lawyer] need a Maharashtra
real-estate lawyer.

## Verdict

**The instinct is right; the shape is wrong.** The money is in land deals, not only in software.
But an open listing portal that takes commission is crowded, conflicts with our core product and
brings heavy regulation. The version that works:

1. **Work for the builder (buy side), never both sides.** Builders already pay for Levels 1–6.
   ScridddHub stays on their side.
2. **Free "Land Health Report" for landowners** as the reason to share their land and papers:
   a valuation, a 5-year infrastructure outlook, and above all **title gaps found before a buyer
   finds them** (a missing mutation entry, a 7/12 that doesn't match the deed, a missing NA order).
   This becomes our supply of parcels.
3. **Charge builders for due-diligence reports** (about ₹25–50k per parcel), co-signed by a
   partner advocate. AI drafts, a lawyer signs and carries the title opinion.
4. **Commission only later, only builder-paid, only under a signed mandate before introduction,**
   and only after MahaRERA agent registration. Test it on 3–5 deals before building any
   marketplace features.
5. **Never a "verified" or "clean title" badge.** Show exactly what was checked, against which
   source, on which date, and who signed it. Seller uploads are labelled "seller-provided,
   unverified" until a person checks them.

## Where the council agreed
- Seller listings should be **free**; they are supply, not revenue.
- The paid product is **verification signed by a named advocate**, not listing.
- **No "verified" badge**, no "clean title", no guarantees.
- Seller uploads prove nothing on their own; they show where to look.
- Gather public data only where there is no captcha (MahaRERA lists, gazettes, DP maps).
  eCourts, IGR and Bhulekh stay manual or seller-provided.

## Where it disagreed: commission
| Member | Position |
|---|---|
| Economics | Commission can work **if the builder pays** and signs a fee mandate **before** we introduce a parcel. Riskiest assumption: will builders sign up front? |
| Skeptic | **No commission on deals where we also do the diligence.** Every red flag we raise would cost us money; builders will notice. Take a flat, disclosed builder fee paid whether or not the deal closes. |
| Market | Don't build a commission portal (1acre already gives free listings in MMR). Take commission only as a hands-on **promoter on a few large deals**, like Cushman & Wakefield or UK land promoters. |
| Legal | Taking commission makes us an agent: MahaRERA firm registration (₹1 lakh), Certificate of Competency for staff, written brokerage agreements, bank escrow for token money. |

**Resolution:** keep the diligence fee separate and paid whatever the outcome, with the title
opinion signed by an outside advocate. That removes most of the skeptic's conflict. Allow a
builder-paid sourcing fee only on parcels we introduced under a prior mandate. Hold off on
commission until registration is done and 3 builders have signed mandates.

## Key facts
- **Competitor: 1acre.in** is a land marketplace with 17K+ geo-referenced listings in 60 cities,
  already listing Shahapur, Karjat and Panvel. It's free for sellers and agents and earns from
  developer subscriptions and selected deal fees. ([about](https://www.1acre.in/about-us),
  [Panvel](https://1acre.in/hi/lands-for-sale/panvel-raigad-maharashtra))
- **Landeed** (title search, 24 states): $8.3M seed in 2023 and $5M in 2025, pay per document.
  **TEAL** (land-records AI, B2B for lenders): $2M from Omidyar and Info Edge.
  ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/article/companies/property-data-start-up-landeed-raises-8-3mn-in-seed-round-funding-123012400486_1.html),
  [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/proptech-startup-teal-raises-fund-led-by-omidyar-network-to-expand-data-engineering-capabilities/))
- **"Agents earn a lot" needs a reality check.**
  - MMR had about **32 organised land deals in 2025** ([NBM&CW](https://www.nbmcw.com/news/mmr-hits-32-land-deals-in-2025-leads-india-with-500-acres-transacted.html)).
  - Land deals take 6–18 months, and many die at the title stage.
  - At 1% per side, a 5-acre Karjat parcel earns about ₹5–10 lakh; a Panvel parcel about ₹36–80 lakh.
  - NoBroker lost about ₹300–411 Cr a year while changing brokerage
    ([IANS](https://ianslive.in/nobroker-clocks-rs-411-crore-loss-in-fy24-as-miscellaneous-dominate-rising-expenses--20250514125104)).
- **Commission norms:** about 1% from each side on resale (0.5% above ₹5 Cr), plus 18% GST. Since
  Oct 2024 MahaRERA requires brokerage to be written into sale agreements.
  ([KS&K](https://ksandk.com/newsletter/maharera-real-estate-agents-landlords/))
- **Joint development is growing:** JLL counted 7 Mumbai JDAs over 62.5 acres in 18 months
  ([Realty Today](https://therealtytoday.com/news/joint-development-agreements-surge-in-real-estate-across-indian-cities-jll-report)).
- **Fraud is systemic, even inside government records.**
  - Pune: 38,027 orders changed 7/12 records over five years, and 15 officials were suspended
    ([FPJ](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/pune/maharashtra-govt-admits-pune-land-scam-orders-statewide-audit)).
  - Tathawade: government land was sold for ₹33 Cr using old 7/12s.
  - Thane: a forged PoA sold a dead man's land
    ([The Week](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/09/15/bes3-mh-land-fraud.html)).
- **Title insurance exists in India** (HDFC ERGO and others), mostly sold to developers. A
  possible referral partner.

## Legal must-dos before any commission [Lawyer]
1. MahaRERA agent registration for the company (₹1 lakh). Certificate of Competency for the
   signatory and deal staff. Registration number on every deal and ad.
   ([RERA s.9](https://courtbook.in/real-estate-regulation-and-development-act-2016/section-9);
   penalty ₹10,000 a day, up to 5% of property cost.)
2. Written opinion on plotted layouts that should be RERA-registered, and on buyer eligibility
   under s.63 of the Tenancy Act (non-agriculturists buying agricultural land, amended April 2026).
3. Written brokerage agreements: trigger, who pays, disclosed to both parties, anti-bypass period.
4. Dated document checklists instead of badges; advocate-signed title reports with professional
   indemnity insurance.
5. DPDP Act (rules notified Nov 2025, full duties by May 2027): itemised consent, masked Aadhaar,
   redacted PAN, retention schedule, breach plan, grievance officer.
6. Token money only through bank or regulated escrow, never in company accounts. A payment
   aggregator cannot also be a marketplace (RBI directions, Sept 2025).

**Red lines:** no captcha bypass or scraping of eCourts or IGR; no "clean title" or guarantees; no
full Aadhaar stored; no brokering plots in layouts that should be RERA-registered; no helping
non-agriculturists buy agricultural land without permission or an exemption.

## What data is needed before buying land, and where it comes from
| Kind | Items |
|---|---|
| Public, no captcha (we can collect) | MahaRERA projects, complaints, CC/plans, progress reports; DP/UDCPR maps (rough); CRZ, forest and ESZ maps (rough); acquisition notices in gazettes |
| Public but captcha (a person fetches, or the seller supplies) | 7/12, 8A, Ferfar, property card, Index-II/IGR (only from 2002 outside Mumbai), eCourts cases, tenure class (I/II), tenancy remarks |
| Seller-provided (self-reported until checked) | NA order, zone certificate, ULC orders, PoA chain, heirship papers, tax receipts |
| Needs a professional | 30-year title search, boundary survey (TILR), possession check, succession and heirs (highest fraud risk) |

## Cheapest tests first (no code)
| Must be true | Test | Kill or continue |
|---|---|---|
| Builders trust a diligence provider that also earns on deals | Ask 10 builder land heads | Fewer than 5 yes: drop commission |
| Builders pay for diligence on its own | Pre-sell 3 parcel reports at ₹25–50k with a partner advocate | Paid within 30 days? |
| Owners share real documents | 20 owners through 2 friendly brokers; audit 5 against Mahabhumi/IGR | Share who upload, and the accuracy |
| Builders sign mandates before introductions | Ask 3 builders to sign | Refusal: no commission revenue |
| Our process catches fakes | Run 10 past disputed parcels from a lawyer's files blind | Catch rate |

## Phased plan
- **Phase 0 (weeks 0–4, mostly manual):**
  - Collect builder "buy boxes" (taluka, size, budget, JDA yes/no).
  - Sign 5 brokers and 1 advocate as partners.
  - Get the lawyer's opinion on registration.
  - Produce the Land Health Report as a PDF from the Level 1 pipeline.
- **Phase 1 (weeks 4–13, one taluka where 3+ builders are buying):**
  - WhatsApp intake, report, match, mandate, site visit, all by hand.
  - Charge builders for reports.
  - 90-day targets: 100 owner intakes, 40% sharing full documents, gaps found in 30%, 3 builder
    mandates, 10 site visits or LOIs, at least 1 paid report.
- **Phase 2 (months 4–12, only if Phase 1 hits):**
  - Owner data room in the app.
  - JDA matching.
  - Referrals to lenders and title insurers.
  - 3 talukas.
  - First builder-paid sourcing fees.

## Fit with the current build
The Legal & RERA check already has the right shape: risks, chain, searches, RERA history,
documents. What a seller data room adds to each item is provenance: "seller-provided" vs
"checked against MahaRERA / Mahabhumi on [date] by [person]". The ownership-chain upload
(`tasks-todo/ownership-chain-from-7-12.md`) is also the core of the Land Health Report.
