# Decision after the fixed development comparison

Keep the comparable-listing baseline as the reference. Ridge and histogram gradient boosting both have higher mean rupee error, median percentage error and group-balanced rupee error on the same 66 held-out listings. Do not select either for production or tune repeatedly against these folds.

Coverage is unchanged: 66 scored and 298 abstained for each model. The results do not establish a reliable Khadakpada rate, transaction value or future-date prediction. All ten serialized fold models are retained only to reproduce the experiment; no full-data production model was fitted.

The next useful step is additional independent, permitted locality evidence with improved parcel grouping and later observation dates. Source access outcomes and remaining work are in the collection-and-ML status document. The current evidence batch is frozen, not complete market coverage.
