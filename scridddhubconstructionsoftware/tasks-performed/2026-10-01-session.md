## Decisions (and why)
- **Valuation: asking price withheld from the AI** (it would anchor the estimate);
  **computed in code:** totals, margin and verdict; **on demand only** (AI cost).
- **No captcha bypass, and no searching for solver services.** The routes that need a person are
  set up so the team does the human step (captcha, RTI submission).
- **CIDCO/MIDC tender crawler deferred:** winning prices appear mostly in news, not structured
  result pages, so it would yield only a few points.
